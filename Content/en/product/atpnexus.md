# ATP Nexus

ATP Nexus is a client app for Bluesky, the social network built on the AT Protocol.
It runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch and Apple Vision Pro, letting you subscribe to your favorite feeds and lists and browse them as timelines tailored to your interests.

This app has the following features

- Browse posts from feeds and lists
- Post, reply, quote and react to posts
- Subscribe to feeds and lists, and create lists
- Multiple windows and multiple accounts
- Continue across devices with Handoff
- Show account status with widgets
- Open links in the in-app browser and view photos and videos
